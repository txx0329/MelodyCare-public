ABOUT THE CENTRE
MelodyCare is a private psychology centre for children, teenagers and parents in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia. We combine psychological assessment, therapy and parent coaching so a family can get several kinds of help in one place. Our approach is patient, ethical and family-centred.

LOCATION AND GETTING HERE
Address: Level 5, Menara Melodi, Jalan Harmoni 3, 47400 Petaling Jaya, Selangor.
The centre is on Level 5. Take the lift from the main lobby. The building is wheelchair accessible and has a baby-changing room on Level 5.

PARKING
Visitor parking is in the building's basement car park (levels B1 and B2). Bring your parking ticket to reception and the first two hours are validated for clients with an appointment. Accessible parking bays are next to the lift lobby on B1. Pick-up and drop-off is possible at the front entrance for a few minutes.

OPENING HOURS
Monday to Friday: 9am to 6pm. Saturday: 9am to 1pm. Closed on Sundays and public holidays. Online sessions can be arranged within these hours.

CONTACT AND BOOKING
Phone: +60 3-1234 5678. WhatsApp: +60 12-345 6789. Email: hello@melodycare.my.
To book, call, WhatsApp or email us and our front desk will arrange a short first conversation. Fees are not published online; please contact us for them.

SERVICES
1. Early Development Programme (EDP): a structured programme for children with developmental, learning or behavioural challenges, planned around each child's pace.
2. Parent Coaching: practical strategies and emotional support for parents.
3. Developmental and Learning Assessment: a full picture of a child's development, learning style and support needs.
4. Counselling and Psychotherapy: therapy with qualified psychologists for children, teenagers and adults.
5. Social Skills Therapy: help for children who find it hard to communicate, interact and make friends.
6. Emotional Skills Workshops: group sessions on emotions, friendship and resilience.
7. Online Sessions: parent coaching, programme sessions and consultations from home.

IF SOMEONE IS IN DISTRESS
If a person or child is in immediate danger, call Helpline HEAL 15555 or the local emergency number straight away. Do not wait for an appointment.
