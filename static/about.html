Care that grows with your child, and with you

MelodyCare is a private psychological care centre for children, teenagers and parents. We take time to understand each child first, then plan support that the whole family can sustain.

Explore our services Book a first conversation
“When a parent is heard, a child is heard. When a family is steady, a child can grow.”
Dr. Lee Mei Ling, founder of MelodyCare

From one small studio to a connected circle of care

MelodyCare began in 2009 with one clinical psychologist and a single therapy room. Her belief was simple: psychological care should be ethical, gentle and respectful of how children really develop.

Today our psychologists, child development specialists and life educators work together, so a family does not have to explain their story again every time they need a different kind of help.

We serve families across Malaysia, in person and online.

  1. 2009First therapy room opens in Petaling Jaya.
  2. 2013Early Development Programme (EDP) launches.

What we hold on to

Three promises we keep with every family, whatever the programme.

Patience over pressure

Children change at their own pace. We plan around the child in front of us, not a timetable.

Ethical care over quick results

We explain what we see, why we suggest it, and what it will ask of you before we begin.

Long-term trust

Families and therapists build a relationship over years. We protect it with honesty and privacy.

Our mission

To give every child and family a safe, professional and compassionate place to understand their challenges and find a way forward.

Our vision

A Malaysia where families can reach good psychological support early, wherever they live.

How we support families

Seven services that can be used on their own or together.

Early Development Programme (EDP)

A structured, evidence-based programme for children with developmental, learning or behavioural challenges.

Parent Coaching

Practical strategies and emotional support for parents, including parents who are struggling themselves.

Developmental and Learning Assessment

A full picture of your child’s development, learning style and areas that need support.

Counselling and Psychotherapy

Evidence-based therapy with qualified psychologists for children, teenagers and adults.

Social Skills Therapy

Help for children who find it hard to communicate, interact and make friends.

Emotional Skills Workshops

Emotional intelligence, social etiquette and resilience, taught in ways children enjoy.

Online Sessions

Parenting coaching, EDP sessions and psychological consultation from home.

Technology gives us time. People give the care.

The MelodyCare Support System lets parents ask questions and find comfort between sessions. It gives our therapists a clearer view of how families are doing, so they can spend more of their time truly seeing the child.

The chatbot is not a therapist and never replaces one.

  • Parents get a calm place to startAnswers are drawn from our own guidance on child mental health and parenting.
